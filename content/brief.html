Jen won 41B, 11,363 to 9,656

  • Unofficial. MN Secretary of State precinct files, last updated 4 Nov 10:48 AM. Certified by the State Canvassing Board on 19 Nov. 54.0% to 45.9%, 33 write-ins, all 15 precincts.
  • Turnout. 21,052 votes in the House race against 24,598 in 2024, a midterm against a presidential year. The governor race drew 21,234 ballots in 41B.
  • The pre-election estimate was low. The 26 Oct review put Jen near 50.8% two-way, margin about +330, one-sigma range 48.0% to 53.5%. She got 54.1% two-way and +1,707, above the top of that range.

1. A 2,217-vote swing from 2024, on 3,546 fewer ballots

2024 was Dippel by 510. The margin moved 10.2 points, from −2.1 to +8.1. Hastings went from −581 to +513, Cottage Grove from +361 to +1,376, Denmark and Nininger from −290 to −182. Five precincts flipped: Cottage Grove P-7 and Hastings W-2 P-3, W-3 P-1, W-3 P-2 and W-4 P-2.

2. Jen ran ahead of every DFL candidate on the 41B ballot

3. The swing was broad

4. What we learned

  • Republican drop-off decided it. Jen kept 95% of her 2024 vote (11,363 against 12,019); Beck got 77% of Dippel's (9,656 against 12,529). Switching against staying home is unknown until voter history loads.
  • About 420 votes look like Jen's own: her margin over Klobuchar's in the same precincts.
  • Her edge was biggest in Republican areas (Nininger +6.2 points over Klobuchar's margin, Denmark and W-1 P-2 +4.3), which suggests crossover voters (inference).
  • Dippel is still strong locally. He ran about 1,150 votes ahead of Beck in 41B.
  • The swing was broad. Every precinct moved toward Jen, by 2 to 16.5 points, so no single program explains it.
  • The estimate missed by about 1,400 votes. Next cycle, check the environment assumption against down-ballot results.

Section 1

Where things stand

Field figures in this section are sample values.

The campaign has knocked more doors than at this point in 2024 and is reaching voters at the same rate. The seat's history says that does not settle it.

Section 2

The race

41B has no incumbent, both parties list it as a top target, and the Minnesota House has 67 DFL members, 66 Republicans and one vacancy (21A, vacant since 21 June 2026). Statewide polls narrowed in September.

2a. The forums

Two forums, no rebuttals in either, and no direct attack between Fox and Beck in either. Water was the shared top issue in Hastings.

Section 3

Past cycles

The Republican margin on this turf fell from 21 points in 2014 to about 2 points in 2018, 2022 and 2024. It has not crossed zero.

Section 4

Ticket splitting

Statewide DFL candidates have carried 41B in both cycles on these lines while the DFL House candidate lost. The House share barely moved when the top of the ticket did.

Section 5

Turnout

The last governor-year electorate in 41B was 19,403 voters, 5,841 fewer than in 2024.

Section 6

Money

On 19 Oct Fox held about five times Beck's cash. Outside groups have reported $61,135 against Fox, nearly all of it HRCC mail, radio and digital, filed under an old Fox committee id so the board's by-candidate page does not show it. Spending against Beck is larger, $224,372.

Visibility

560 households have a sign as of 26 Oct, carrying 578 signs, and one request is open. By precinct, Cottage Grove holds 145 of the 524 delivered households inside 41B (27.7%, up from 23.5% on 30 Sep).

Section 9

Digital

Website traffic tripled on 16 Sep, the day paid ads started, and fell back through the first half of October. The week of 19 Oct was up 30% (402 sessions against 310), the best week since late September, with no newsletter on its two biggest days. The site still produces almost no form signups. The email list is small, steady and well read.

Absentee voting has been open since 18 Sep. The next public data on money arrives eight days before the election.

Section 11

Findings and concerns

Six items, each tied to public or campaign numbers above. These are findings and open questions, not advice.

  1. The House line has been flat while the top of the ticket moved. The DFL House candidate took 48.91% of the two-way vote in 2022 and 48.96% in 2024. In 2024 the DFL Senate candidate carried 41B by 2,765 and Fox lost it by 510.
  2. The public page is current through Election Day. As of 13 Oct it carries the Wednesday and Thursday knocks, the 17 Oct kick-off with Rep. Craig and Sen. Seeberger, the 21 Oct knock with Leader Stephenson and the daily get-out-the-vote shifts, all signing up through Mobilize; the Saturday and Sunday series were replaced by the daily shifts.
  3. The site gets the clicks and not the signups. Sessions went from about 25 to 78 a day after 16 Sep, 54 a day in early October and 39 a day in the week of 6 Oct, with no ad running. One form submission came in across 1,167 sessions from 16 to 30 Sep, and none from 27 Sep until 27 Oct. Who runs the Simpli.fi and Google Ads buys, and what they target?
  4. Email opens are high and Issue 6 broke the click slump; the list is flat. Opens run 46% to 66% on the sends with a full read. Clicks were 1 or 2 a send from 21 Sep until Issue 6 on 5 Oct drew 11. The list is 130 on 1 Nov against 128 on 31 Aug, after about 2,800 site sessions since then.
  5. Signs are done as a program. 560 households carry 578 signs on 26 Oct, 153 of the households in Cottage Grove (27%); one request is open. Pickup is planned for Wed 4 Nov.
  6. Outside money came late, as it did in 2024. 82% of 2024's outside money in 41B came after 15 Sep. Through 19 Oct, $61,135 has been reported against Fox and $224,372 against Beck, and Fox held $80,214 in cash against Beck's $15,157.
