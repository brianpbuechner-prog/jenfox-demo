Jen Fox for House 41B

Yard sign app

Requests, delivery routes and the post-election pickup, in one app

What it did, Aug to Nov 2026

  • Replaced competing spreadsheets with one list: requests from the website, phone and events landed in one place and were routed automatically.
  • Volunteers opened a route link on their phone, no sign-in: next stop, one-tap directions, Delivered or Picked up.
  • 560 households had a Jen Fox sign by Election Day, and the same app ran the Nov 4 pickup across 41B.
